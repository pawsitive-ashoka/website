Stockings is the South Bombay puppy princess of Gate 1. A professional nap enthusiast, she loves digging little holes in the ground and curling up in them for long, uninterrupted sleeps. In fact, she takes her rest so seriously that even food placed right in front of her often isn't enough to convince her to get up. Stockings has a habit of dozing off in the middle of the road, an unfortunate talent that has led to multiple vehicular accidents. Yet, time and again, she has proven just how resilient she is, bouncing back every single time with the same sleepy charm intact.
