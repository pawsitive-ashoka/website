Ice Cream is what happens when you max out the affection and physicality slider on a dog. Initially at gate 1 but now found at gate 2, Ice Cream will make maximum body contact with you at any given moment. He doesn't like attention, he loves affection, and will push his snout as far into your stomach as caninely possible. He's a brave boy and is currently at odds with Sultan and his lackeys at gate 2, giving that group of much larger dogs a tough fight. Ice Cream came to Ashoka in September with a bad case of mange, from which he has made a strong recovery. We are hoping his canine-pattern baldness recovers as well.
