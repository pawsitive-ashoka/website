Big Boi, as the name suggests, is a large, floofy dog with doll-like eyes and the presence of someone who has seen it all. As old as time itself, he carries his age with remarkable grace and hasn’t lost an ounce of his spirit. Though he has cataracts and limited vision, it does little to slow him down or dampen his confidence. Call his name and Big Boi will run toward the sound of your voice, stopping right in front of you and patiently waiting for attention. With humans, he is calm, gentle, and wise, an old soul in every sense. But when his boundaries are crossed or his territory is threatened, he is quick to remind everyone that he is not to be underestimated. Despite his age, Big Boi is famously fast and determined, holding his own against anything that challenges him.
